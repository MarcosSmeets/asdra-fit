# Prompts finais das concept sheets

As tres imagens foram geradas pelo modo built-in do `imagegen`, com
`apps/mobile/assets/adaris/sheets/adari-action-atlas-v2.png` como imagem de
referencia. O fundo magenta e proposital para facilitar recorte e comparacao.

## Terravok

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production character evolution concept sheet for the mobile RPG Ad Sidera
Input image: authoritative style and identity reference; use only its top row Brontu as the base-form reference
Primary request: create exactly four separate full-body quadruped forms in one horizontal row, showing Brontu unchanged, then Brontar, Bronterra, and Asterhorn as a clear continuous evolution from compact loyal rock guardian to immense astral mountain protector
Subject progression: preserve slate segmented hide, golden eyes, amber crystal shoulders, short mineral tail and glowing seams; Brontar gains raised shoulders, small swept horn buds and restrained gold-tech foreleg plates; Bronterra gains a deep chest, broad horns, integrated shield-like chest and foreleg slabs, and connected gold circuits; Asterhorn becomes a colossal low quadruped with complete segmented astral armor, constellation marks and branching star-horns whose negative space suggests a star
Style/medium: original detailed 32-bit pixel art matching the reference, crisp visible pixel clusters, restrained local-color 1-pixel outlines, hard shadows, no smooth painting
Composition/framing: orthographic three-quarter view facing left, same ground line and camera, evenly separated forms, controlled size progression, generous padding, no overlap, no cropping
Lighting/mood: consistent warm top-left light, mature cosmic RPG tone
Color palette: midnight and slate dominant, stellar gold and light-gold crystal accents, sparse stellar-white points
Materials/textures: matte basalt, faceted amber crystal, brushed astral metal
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #FF00FF chroma-key background with no floor plane, shadow, scenery, gradient or texture; do not use #FF00FF on characters
Constraints: exactly four characters, quadrupeds at every stage, preserve family identity, no embedded aura backdrop, no props, no text, labels, numbers, panel borders, logos, watermark, copyrighted-character resemblance, humanoid armor, wings, literal star attached to the head, excessive spikes or cropped horns.
```

Edicao aplicada: remover somente a estrela literal flutuante entre os chifres
de Asterhorn, preservando todas as demais formas e detalhes.
